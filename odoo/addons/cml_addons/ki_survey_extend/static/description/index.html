ki_survey_extend

This module allow you to add "Terms & Conditions" before starting the survey and "Survey tags" on label & answer records that has been created during survey.

Navigate to "User Confirmation" field in survey form view on "Option" page and enable that field.


Navigate to "Survey User Confirmation Content" in "Website Settings" and add the content.


Click on the "Share and invite by email" button on survey form that will open following popup.
Open the highlighted link for survey page.


If "User Confirmation" is enabled on survey form then hightlighted block will apear.
User will not be able to take survey until checkbox for "I accept terms and conditions." is checked.


Navigate to "Survey Tags" from Survey > Configuration to make the record of survey tag.


Add the "Survey Tag" in your labels.


After completing survey "Survey Tag" on answer will be automatically set based on the tag set on the "Row Answer" label.
